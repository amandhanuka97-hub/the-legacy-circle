Article V · The Charter

Five things the Circle will never do.

i.

Never promise assured returns.

Markets do not give guarantees, so neither do we. Anyone who does is selling you something else.

ii.

Never run a deal-of-the-day culture.

Three to four curated opportunities a quarter, matched to your profile. If nothing clears our bar, you hear nothing, and that silence is the service.

iii.

Never hide who should not invest.

Every memo ends by naming the investor profile that should decline it. Saying no is part of what you pay us for.

iv.

Never bury our conflicts.

When an opportunity is originated by our own healthcare investment banking arm, the memo says so in bold, along with exactly how we are compensated. Participation is always optional.

v.

Never speak of your affairs.

Your membership, your holdings and your conversations are private. Discretion is not a feature of the Circle. It is the Circle.
